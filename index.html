Biscoff Cheesecake

Serves 10 | Chill: 4 hours

Crust

  • 1.5 packs Biscoff Biscuits
  • 1/2 cup Butter

Filling

  • 2 tubs Sundale Cream Cheese
  • 1.75 cups Sundale Spoonable Cream
  • 1 tbsp Vanilla Essence & 1 Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Biscoff Spread
  • 0.75g Gelatin Powder

Topping

  • 0.5 cup Biscoff Spread
  • 4 tbsp Hot Water
  • 0.25g Gelatin Powder

Method

  • Blend 1.5 packs of Biscoff biscuits fine, mix with melted butter. Press firmly into springform pan base.
  • Whisk Sundale cream cheese until smooth, add Sundale spoonable cream.
  • Add vanilla essence, lemon juice/zest, sugar, spread, and gelatin powder; mix well.
  • Spoon onto crust, spread evenly.
  • Mix topping spread, hot water, and gelatin until smooth. Fold in cream for marble effect.
  • Pour over filling and refrigerate for 4 hours until set.

Persian Cream Cheese Log

Serves 4 | Prep: 15 mins | Freeze: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sundale Cream Cheese
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Zest
  • 1/2 cup Pistachio Nuts (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup Dried Cranberries (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Date Syrup
  • Salt & Pepper to taste

Method

  • Place Sundale Cream Cheese into a mixing bowl and soften with the back of a spoon.
  • Add lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Whisk until well combined.
  • Scoop mixture into silicone moulds, smooth top. Freeze for 2 hours.
  • Remove from moulds; let thaw slightly. Mix chopped pistachio nuts and dried cranberries.
  • Roll logs into the nut mixture to coat, leaving sides clean.
  • Drizzle with date syrup and serve with biscuits.

Vanilla Panna Cotta

Serves 4 | Prep: 20 mins | Chill: 3-4 hours

Base

  • 1 cup Sundale Full Cream Milk
  • 1 cup Sundale Spoonable Cream
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Powdered Gelatine & 1/2c Water
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Paste

Topping

  • 2 cups Fresh Strawberries
  • 2 tbsp Sugar & 2 tbsp Water
  • 2 tbsp Balsamic Glaze

Method

  • Heat milk, spoonable cream, sugar & vanilla gently to simmer.
  • Bloom gelatine in water for 5 min, then whisk into hot mixture to dissolve.
  • Strain mixture, cool slightly, pour into moulds. Refrigerate 3-4 hours.
  • Blend 1 cup strawberries with sugar and water. Simmer on low heat to reduce. Cool.
  • Top set panna cottas with coulis and diced strawberries.
