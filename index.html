Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sundale Milk
  • 1.5 cups flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp Sundale butter

Method

  • Sift dry ingredients.
  • Whisk milk, egg, and melted butter.
  • Fold wet into dry gently.
  • Pour portions onto pan.
  • Flip when bubbles pop.

Dinner

Garlic Pasta

Prep: 5 mins | Cook: 12 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Sundale Milk
  • 250g Pasta
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp Sundale Butter
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan

Method

  • Boil pasta in water.
  • Sauté garlic in butter.
  • Stir in milk & simmer.
  • Toss pasta in sauce.
  • Top with cheese.

Dessert

Malva Pudding

Prep: 15 mins | Bake: 45 mins

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup Sundale Milk
  • 1 cup Sugar & 1 Egg
  • 1 tbsp Apricot Jam
  • 1 cup Flour
  • 1 tsp Baking Soda

Method

  • Beat egg, sugar and jam.
  • Sift dry ingredients.
  • Fold alternating with milk.
  • Bake at 180°C for 45m.
  • Pour hot sauce over.

Main

Creamy Chicken

Prep: 10 mins | Cook: 20 mins

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup Sundale Milk
  • 2 Chicken Breasts
  • 1 cup Mushrooms
  • 1 tbsp Sundale Butter
  • 1 tsp Mixed Herbs

Method

  • Sear chicken in butter.
  • Remove and sauté veg.
  • Add milk, herbs, simmer.
  • Return chicken to thicken.
  • Serve hot.
