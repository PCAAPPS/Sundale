Biscoff Cheesecake

Serves 10 | Chill: 4 hours

Crust

  • 1.5 packs Biscoff Biscuits
  • 1/2 cup Butter

Filling

  • 2 tubs Sundale Cream Cheese
  • 1.75 cups Sundale Spoonable Cream
  • 1 tbsp Vanilla Essence
  • 1 Lemon (juice & zest)
  • 4 tbsp Brown Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Salt (divided)
  • 1 tbsp Biscoff Spread
  • 0.75g Gelatin Powder
  • 0.5 pack Biscoff Biscuits (for filling)

Topping

  • 0.5 cup Biscoff Spread
  • 4 tbsp Hot Water
  • 0.25 tsp Salt
  • 0.25g Gelatin Powder
  • 2 tbsp Sundale Spoonable Cream
  • Remaining Biscoff Biscuits

Method

  • Blend 1.5 packs of Biscoff biscuits until fine, add melted butter and blend. Pour into 30cm springform pan lined with cling wrap; compress with spatula and glass base.
  • Filling: Whisk Sundale cream cheese until smooth, add Sundale spoonable cream and whisk.
  • Add vanilla essence, lemon juice & zest, brown sugar, half the salt, Biscoff spread, and gelatin powder; mix well.
  • Spoon 3/4 of filling onto crust, break leftover biscuits into filling, add rest of filling and spread evenly without gaps.
  • Topping: Mix Biscoff spread, hot water, remaining salt, and gelatin powder until smooth. Fold in 2 tbsp spoonable cream for a marbled effect.
  • Pour over filling, break remaining biscuits over top center, and refrigerate for 4 hours until set.

Persian Cream Cheese Log

Serves 4 | Prep: 15 mins | Freeze: 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sundale Cream Cheese
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Zest
  • 1/2 cup Pistachio Nuts (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup Dried Cranberries (chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Date Syrup
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste

Method

  • Place Sundale Cream Cheese into a mixing bowl and soften with the back of a spoon.
  • Add lemon zest, salt, and pepper. Whisk until well combined.
  • Scoop the cream cheese mixture into silicone moulds, fill with no gaps, smooth top, and scrape excess.
  • Freeze for 2 hours or until set.
  • Remove from moulds; let thaw slightly. Mix chopped pistachio nuts and dried cranberries together.
  • Roll logs into the nut and cranberry mixture to coat, leaving sides clean to show the cream cheese.
  • Drizzle with date syrup, garnish with edible flowers and microgreens, and serve with pretzels or biscuits.

Vanilla Panna Cotta

Serves 4 | Prep: 20 mins | Chill: 3-4 hours

Ingredients (Panna Cotta)

  • 1 cup Sundale Full Cream Milk
  • 1 cup Sundale Spoonable Cream
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1 tbsp Powdered Gelatine
  • 1/2 cup Water
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Paste

Ingredients (Topping)

  • 2 cups Fresh Strawberries
  • 2 tbsp Sugar & 2 tbsp Water
  • 2 tbsp Balsamic Glaze

Method

  • In a medium pot, add milk, spoonable cream, sugar & vanilla paste. Heat gently to simmer (do not boil). Whisk to combine.
  • Add powdered gelatine to water, mix and set aside to bloom for 5 min.
  • Once simmering, add bloomed gelatin & whisk to dissolve. Simmer for 5 min until gelatin has completely dissolved.
  • Turn off heat & strain mixture. Cool slightly. Pour equally into moulds on tray. Refrigerate 3-4 hours until set.
  • Topping: place 1 cup strawberries into blender with sugar and water, blend until smooth. Pour coulis into saucepan & simmer on low heat for 5 min to reduce. Cool.
  • Cut rest of strawberries into small cubes.
  • Top set panna cottas with coulis, then strawberry cubes.
  • Complete with a drizzle of glaze over the strawberries.
