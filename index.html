Biscoff Cheesecake

Prep: 20 mins | Chill: 4 hours

Ingredients

  • 250g Biscoff cookies, crushed
  • 100g Sundale Butter, melted
  • 500g Cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup Sundale Double Greek Yoghurt
  • 1 cup Biscoff spread
  • 1 tsp Vanilla extract

Method

  • Mix crushed cookies with melted Sundale butter and press into base.
  • Beat cream cheese, Sundale double Greek yoghurt, and vanilla until smooth.
  • Fold in melted Biscoff spread gently.
  • Pour mixture over the prepared crust.
  • Chill in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours before serving.

Persian Cream Cheese Log

Prep: 15 mins

Ingredients

  • 400g Cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp Sundale Butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup Pistachios, chopped
  • 1/4 cup Dried cranberries, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Fresh herbs (parsley/mint)
  • Pinch of salt and black pepper

Method

  • In a bowl, mix cream cheese and softened Sundale butter until creamy.
  • Shape the mixture into a neat log using parchment paper.
  • Roll the log in chopped pistachios, cranberries, and fresh herbs.
  • Wrap tightly and refrigerate for 2 hours to firm up.
  • Serve chilled with crackers and warm bread.

Dessert

Vanilla Panna Cotta

Prep: 15 mins | Chill: 3 hours

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups Sundale Fresh Milk
  • 1.5 cups Heavy cream
  • 1/3 cup Sugar
  • 2 tsp Gelatin powder
  • 1 Vanilla bean, split

Method

  • Sprinkle gelatin over 3 tbsp cold Sundale milk and let bloom.
  • In a saucepan, heat cream, sugar, and vanilla bean until warm.
  • Whisk in the dissolved gelatin until completely melted.
  • Pour into molds and chill for 3 hours until set.
  • Unmold onto plates and garnish with fresh berries.
